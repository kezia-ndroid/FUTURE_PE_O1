# Structured Prompts — GlowRoots Skincare Website Copy

---

## BUSINESS CONTEXT BLOCK
*(Include this at the top of every prompt below)*

```
BUSINESS DETAILS:
- Business name: GlowRoots Skincare
- Business type: D2C skincare brand
- Location: Kerala, India (ships pan-India)
- Product: Natural face serum — niacinamide + turmeric
- Target audience: Indian women aged 18–32, dealing with pigmentation, dark spots, dull skin
- USP: Clean ingredients, no parabens, no fragrance, affordable (₹399), Ayurvedic roots
- Tone: Warm, trustworthy, science-backed but simple. Never salesy.
- Sentence rule: Max 15 words per sentence. Benefit-first, feature-second.
```

---

## Prompt 1 — Homepage Copy

```
Using the BUSINESS DETAILS above, write homepage copy for GlowRoots Skincare website.

Generate the following sections:

1. HERO HEADLINE
   - One powerful line (max 10 words)
   - Must communicate the main benefit, not the product name
   - Should stop a scrolling visitor instantly

2. SUB-HEADLINE
   - One sentence (max 18 words)
   - Answer: who is this for + what will they get

3. INTRO SECTION (40–60 words)
   - Write in second person ("you / your skin")
   - Acknowledge the problem first (pigmentation, failed products, confusion)
   - Then position GlowRoots as the clean, honest solution
   - Do NOT list ingredients here

4. VALUE PROPOSITION BLOCK (3 short points)
   - Each point: one bold label + one sentence
   - Focus on: clean ingredients, results, affordability
   - Keep each point under 15 words

5. SOCIAL PROOF LINE
   - One line that feels like a real stat or customer quote
   - Example format: "X customers across India switched to GlowRoots in the last Y months"

Do not use the words: revolutionary, game-changing, best-in-class, unlock, journey.
```

---

## Prompt 2 — Services / Products Page Copy

```
Using the BUSINESS DETAILS above, write the products/services page copy for GlowRoots Skincare.

GlowRoots has 3 products:
1. Natural Glow Serum (hero product — niacinamide + turmeric, ₹399)
2. Hydra-Calm Moisturiser (for sensitive skin, fragrance-free, ₹349)
3. Glow Starter Kit (serum + moisturiser bundle, ₹699)

For EACH product, generate:
- Product name (keep existing name)
- One-line tagline (max 10 words, benefit-focused)
- Description (50–70 words, conversational, second person)
- 3 key benefits (bullet format, each under 10 words)
- Who it's best for (one sentence)
- Price display line (e.g. "₹399 · Free shipping above ₹499")

Tone: Like a knowledgeable friend recommending something — honest, warm, not pushy.
Do NOT use: "luxurious", "indulge", "pamper", "transform overnight".
```

---

## Prompt 3 — CTA Sections

```
Using the BUSINESS DETAILS above, write 4 CTA sections for the GlowRoots Skincare website.

CTA 1 — MAIN SHOP CTA (after hero section)
- Heading: max 8 words
- Supporting line: max 15 words
- Button text: 2–4 words (not just "Buy Now")

CTA 2 — TRUST-BUILDING CTA (mid-page, after product section)
- Include 3 trust signals (e.g. ingredient transparency, no-questions return policy, cruelty-free)
- Each trust signal: icon label + one short sentence
- Closing CTA button: 3–5 words

CTA 3 — URGENCY / OFFER CTA (near bottom of page)
- Promote a limited offer (create a believable one — e.g. starter kit discount, free shipping week)
- Heading + 1 supporting line + button
- Must feel real, not fake-urgent

CTA 4 — CONTACT / COMMUNITY CTA (footer area)
- Invite visitors to follow on Instagram or join a WhatsApp community
- Warm, friendly tone
- Heading + 1 line + 2 button options (Shop / Follow us)

Do not use: "Don't miss out", "Limited time only!!!", "Act now".
```

## Prompt 4 — Tone Adaptation (Reusable for Other Business Types)

```
I need to adapt the GlowRoots website copy prompts above for a different business type.

New business details:
[REPLACE WITH: business name, type, location, audience, USP, tone]

Using the same 3-prompt structure (Homepage / Services / CTAs):
- Keep all section structures identical
- Replace all skincare-specific language with language relevant to [NEW BUSINESS TYPE]
- Adjust tone from "warm skincare friend" to [NEW TONE — e.g. "professional clinic", "energetic gym"]

Output all 3 sections sequentially.
```

---

## Prompt Engineering Notes

| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Added "max 15 words per sentence" rule | Prevented long, corporate-sounding sentences |
| Listed banned words per prompt | Reduced generic AI filler by ~60% |
| Used "second person (you/your)" instruction | Made copy feel personal, not like a brochure |
| Gave product names + prices upfront | AI used accurate details instead of placeholders |
| Separated prompts by page section | Easier to regenerate one section without redoing all |
